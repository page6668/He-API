# Risk Profile: Story 7.6

**Story Title:** 微信支付 WeChat Pay HK / Cross-border (WeChat Pay HK / cross-border payment channel)
**Assessment Date:** 2026-06-10
**Assessed By:** Turing (Test Architect / QA)
**Story Complexity:** 5/7 (api_changes, new_patterns, cross_service, core_docs, data_sync; db_schema + performance = false)
**Scope:** **Scoped** (Decision 8B). The exactly-once credit spine, the `recharge_orders` state-machine, the amount-integrity guard **(incl. the 7.5 paid-currency fix)**, the `payment.completed` producer, the provider-agnostic `credit.go` applier **(incl. its `toUSD()` branch)**, the Redis mirror, and the PCI no-PAN boundary are **REUSED VERBATIM** and already adversarially proven by 7.3 (62 scenarios) + 7.4 (54) + 7.5 (66). Inherited-and-proven risks therefore score a **low residual probability**; this profile concentrates on the **genuinely-new wechat surface**: ⭐ the platform's FIRST **AES-256-GCM `resource` decrypt**, the WeChat APIv3 asymmetric verify with **`Wechatpay-Serial` platform-cert selection**, and the FIRST **HKD→USD fx-at-credit** exercise.

> **What changed vs 7.5's risk shape.** 7.5's #1 risk was the constructed-string **canonicalisation trap** (`r.URL.Path` vs the configured notify path — score **16**). That risk is **eliminated** in 7.6: the WeChat APIv3 signed string is exactly `<Wechatpay-Timestamp>\n<Wechatpay-Nonce>\n<rawBody>\n` — **no method/URI component**, so there is no URI to canonicalise and no `r.URL.Path` trap. In its place, two **new encryption-layer risks** arrive (the AES-GCM decrypt must be fail-closed; the decrypt must never run on unverified input) plus the **`Wechatpay-Serial` selection** vector (an unknown serial must reject, not silently accept). The fx risk also **drops** vs 7.5 (DATA-002): 7.5 *fixed* the paid-currency guard and *built* the minor-unit table; 7.6 inherits both unchanged and only routes a new currency through them.

## Executive Summary

**Total Risks Identified:** 15
**Risk Distribution:**
- Critical (20-25): **0**
- High (13-19): **3**
- Medium (6-12): **9**
- Low (1-5): **3**

> **Why no Critical risk despite a payment channel + a brand-new crypto primitive.** The two highest-*impact* paths — webhook forgery (mint free balance) and double-credit — are capped at *Possible*/*Very-unlikely* probability respectively, because (a) the verify-before-anything handler + the exactly-once state-machine are **inherited and proven** (reused unchanged), (b) the asymmetric signature means an attacker cannot forge without WeChat's platform private key, and (c) every new failure mode is **fail-closed by design** — a bad signature, an unknown serial, an AES-GCM tag mismatch, a stale timestamp, a minor-unit scale error, or a missing HKD rate all → reject/park, never a credit. The residual risk is concentrated in *correctness* of the NEW provider impl (esp. the hand-rolled AES-GCM decrypt), not in an open fraud vector.

**Top 3 Risks:**
1. **SEC-001**: ⭐ AES-256-GCM `resource` decrypt not fail-closed (unchecked `gcm.Open` error / partial-plaintext parse) → a tampered or wrong-key resource yields a parsed transaction → wrong/forged credit (Score: **15**, High)
2. **SEC-002**: Forged-callback acceptance via an asymmetric-verify impl bug — incl. an **unknown `Wechatpay-Serial` silently accepted** (verification skipped) → minted free balance (Score: **15**, High)
3. **DATA-001**: Minor-unit mis-scale → 100× over/under credit (Score: **15**, High)

## Detailed Risk Analysis

### SEC-001: AES-256-GCM Resource Decrypt Not Fail-Closed (⭐ the new-primitive integrity fence)

**Category:** Security/Data · **Probability:** 3 (Possible) · **Impact:** 5 (Critical) · **Score:** 15 (High)

**Description:**
WeChat APIv3 callbacks deliver the payment result ENCRYPTED — the platform's FIRST encrypted webhook body. The inner `resource` is `{algorithm:"AEAD_AES_256_GCM", ciphertext:<b64>, nonce:<12B>, associated_data:"transaction"}`, decrypted with the symmetric `APIV3Key` via `gcm.Open(nil, nonce, b64decode(ciphertext), associated_data)`. The 16-byte GCM auth tag is the **second** integrity guard under the asymmetric signature. The risk is a hand-rolled decrypt that is NOT fail-closed: ignoring the `gcm.Open` error and parsing whatever bytes come back, manually splitting/handling the trailing tag (Go's `gcm.Open` consumes it — a manual split is a classic bug), wiring the wrong nonce or omitting the AAD, or treating a decrypt error as "empty body → skip" instead of "reject". Any of these could let a tampered ciphertext or a wrong-key payload yield a "parsed" transaction → a wrong or forged credit. This is the genuinely-new attack surface with **no prior-channel analogue** (Stripe/Coinbase HMAC + Alipay+ RSA all signed *plaintext*).

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- **Prevention:** Use Go stdlib `crypto/cipher.NewGCM` + `gcm.Open` (NEVER hand-split the tag); pass `nonce` and `associated_data` as the authenticated inputs exactly; on **any** `gcm.Open` error → fail-closed (return a reject/`ErrSignatureInvalid`-class error, NO credit, NEVER parse a partial plaintext); validate the `APIV3Key` is 32 bytes at construction (UNIT-005); decrypt ONLY after the signature verifies (SEC-004).
- **Detection:** P0 unit lane — happy decrypt (7.6-UNIT-030), tampered ciphertext (7.6-UNIT-031), tampered nonce/AAD (7.6-UNIT-032), **wrong `APIV3Key`** (7.6-UNIT-033); the encryption-layer no-side-effect integration (7.6-INT-027).
- **Response:** A decrypt failure parks/rejects (no money action); an anomaly in the reject metric triggers a security/crypto review.
- **Owner:** Dev

**Test Requirements:** 7.6-UNIT-030/031/032/033, 7.6-INT-027

**References:** Q-ENCRYPT; AC3 BR-W-3; Dev Notes "AES-256-GCM `resource` decrypt"; `[[project_toolchain_env_limits]]` (in-test random 32B key + GCM fixtures)

### SEC-002: Forged-Callback Acceptance — incl. Unknown-Serial Skip (the fraud fence)

**Category:** Security · **Probability:** 3 (Possible) · **Impact:** 5 (Critical) · **Score:** 15 (High)

**Description:**
The webhook route is mounted outside the bearer/CSRF chain — the WeChat signature is the **sole credential**. A bug in the NEW asymmetric verify would let a forged `TRANSACTION.SUCCESS` mint free balance. Failure modes: decrypting/parsing the body before verifying (SEC-004), a permissive fallback when `Wechatpay-Signature` is malformed/absent, verifying against the wrong key, or — the **NEW vector** — selecting the platform public key by `Wechatpay-Serial` and *silently skipping verification* (or defaulting to "accept") when the serial does not match the configured platform cert. The serial MUST select a key; an unknown serial MUST **reject**, not bypass. Probability is *Possible* (not *Likely*) because the asymmetric RSA-SHA256 family was de-risked by 7.5 and the verify-before-act handler is inherited; the residual is an impl defect in `wechat.VerifyWebhook`, concentrated in the new serial-selection branch.

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- **Prevention:** `VerifyWebhook` MUST `rsa.VerifyPKCS1v15(platformPublicKey[serial], crypto.SHA256, sha256(<ts>\n<nonce>\n<rawBody>\n), sig)` and return `provider.ErrSignatureInvalid` (decrypting/parsing **nothing**) BEFORE any body processing (BR-W-1); reject absent/malformed `Wechatpay-Signature/Timestamp/Nonce/Serial`; an unknown/mismatched `Wechatpay-Serial` → reject (fail-closed, do NOT skip).
- **Detection:** Adversarial P0 unit lane — forged/absent/**wrong-key** (7.6-UNIT-021/022/023), **unknown serial** (7.6-UNIT-024), signed-string tamper (7.6-UNIT-025); integration + E2E no-side-effect proofs (7.6-INT-022, 7.6-E2E-020); `payment_webhook_rejected{provider:"wechat",reason:"signature"}` metric + slog.
- **Response:** Forged attempts rejected at 400 with zero side effects; a reject-metric anomaly triggers a security review.
- **Owner:** Dev

**Test Requirements:** 7.6-UNIT-021/022/023/024/025, 7.6-INT-022, 7.6-E2E-020

**References:** AC3 BR-W-1/W-2; Q-CERT-ROTATION; PCI SAQ-A boundary; 4.7 `/public/*` precedent

### DATA-001: Minor-Unit Mis-Scale (the 100× defect)

**Category:** Data/Financial · **Probability:** 3 (Possible) · **Impact:** 5 (Critical) · **Score:** 15 (High)

**Description:**
WeChat `amount.total` is an **integer in the currency's minor units** (`"5000"` = 50.00). A hardcoded `/100`, a wrong per-currency scale for the newly-added HKD, or silent truncation of a sub-minor-unit input produces a credit 100× too large or too small — a direct financial-integrity defect on both the outbound Native create (over-charge) and the inbound credit (over-credit). Probability is held at *Possible* despite the table being inherited from 7.5, because HKD is a **newly-routed** currency and a mis-invocation for a new code path is the classic regression; the impact is catastrophic so it is not downgraded.

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- **Prevention:** EXTEND the 7.5 **per-currency minor-unit table** with HKD (USD/HKD/CNY = 2 digits — NOT a hardcoded `/100`), string-decimal end-to-end (`shopspring/decimal`), and **fail-closed** on any scale loss/non-representable value → park + alert, never a mis-scaled credit (BR-W-8, Q-AMOUNT-UNITS).
- **Detection:** P0 unit round-trip (7.6-UNIT-006: `"50.00"↔"5000"`, `"390.00"↔"39000"`, `"350.00"↔"35000"`), fail-closed boundary (7.6-UNIT-007, 7.6-BLIND-BOUNDARY-005), callback-side parse-fail (7.6-UNIT-016), and the integration mis-scale park (7.6-BLIND-DATA-005).
- **Response:** A parked order surfaces via alert for manual reconciliation; no wrong credit is ever applied.
- **Owner:** Dev

**Test Requirements:** 7.6-UNIT-006/007/015/016, 7.6-BLIND-BOUNDARY-005, 7.6-BLIND-DATA-005

**References:** Q-AMOUNT-UNITS; AC2 BR-C-3; AC3 BR-W-8

### SEC-003: Replay — Wechatpay-Timestamp Freshness

**Category:** Security · **Probability:** 3 (Possible) · **Impact:** 4 (High) · **Score:** 12 (Medium)

**Description:**
A captured valid `SUCCESS` callback re-POSTed to double-credit. Posture is on par with 7.5/Stripe: the callback carries `Wechatpay-Timestamp`, enabling a freshness window. Risk is that the window is not enforced or set too wide, leaving the state-machine as the sole fence. Defence-in-depth requires both layers.

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- **Prevention:** Reject a callback whose `Wechatpay-Timestamp` is outside a bounded skew (layer 1, inject `WithClock`); the inherited `recharge_orders` pending→paid state-machine neutralises any within-window replay (layer 2).
- **Detection:** 7.6-UNIT-027 (stale → reject), 7.6-UNIT-028 (window boundary), 7.6-INT-023 (within-window → one credit), 7.6-INT-024 (after-window → 400).
- **Response:** Replays are rejected (stale) or no-op (state-machine); exactly-once holds.
- **Owner:** Dev

**Test Requirements:** 7.6-UNIT-027/028, 7.6-INT-023/024

**References:** AC3 BR-W-5

### SEC-006: Merchant Private-Key Leak (broad blast radius)

**Category:** Security · **Probability:** 2 (Unlikely) · **Impact:** 5 (Critical) · **Score:** 10 (Medium)

**Description:**
`WECHAT_PAY_MERCHANT_PRIVATE_KEY` signs ALL our outbound WeChat calls — leaking it lets an attacker impersonate **us** to WeChat (create/close/refund payments), a broad blast radius. Distinct from the platform public verify cert (a public key) and the symmetric `APIV3Key` (SEC-005). ⚠️ 7.6 holds **TWO** high-value secrets, so secret discipline is doubly important here.

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- **Prevention:** Env-injected only, NEVER logged (generic error wrapping — the 7.3/7.4/7.5 secret-discipline cascade); registered in `docs/dev/secrets`; the merchant private signing key, the platform public verify cert (+serial), the `APIV3Key`, and the `MCH_ID` are DISTINCT config items.
- **Detection:** 7.6-UNIT-035 (no private key / `APIV3Key` / platform key in any error or log); secret-scanning in CI.
- **Response:** Rotate the merchant key on any suspected leak.
- **Owner:** Dev

**Test Requirements:** 7.6-UNIT-035

**References:** Q-SIG; AC3 BR-W-7; `[[project_toolchain_env_limits]]`

### Medium Risks (summary)

- **SEC-004 (8) — Decrypt oracle / order-of-operations:** decrypt MUST run only after the signature verifies (verify→decrypt→parse); an unverified request must never reach `gcm.Open`. Prevents a decrypt oracle and ensures a forged outer request is rejected before any crypto on the ciphertext. Tests: **7.6-UNIT-034** (forged sig + valid ciphertext → rejected before decrypt), 7.6-UNIT-020.
- **SEC-005 (8) — `APIV3Key` leak (NEW symmetric secret):** decrypts every callback resource; a leak exposes callback contents (amounts, txn ids) but does NOT alone enable forgery (the callback signature is asymmetric). Env-injected, never logged. Tests: 7.6-UNIT-035.
- **DATA-002 (8) — HKD→USD fx-at-credit:** drive the inherited `toUSD()` unchanged (HALF-UP, moneyScale=4); compare settled-vs-intent in the PAID currency BEFORE conversion (Q-FX-INTENT, the 7.5 fix — inherited); require an HKD `fx_rate` row (fail-closed park if absent). ⚠️ **ZERO `credit.go` change** is the clean-reuse invariant — an edit here is itself a defect. Tests: 7.6-INT-012/013/014/017, 7.6-INT-018 (CNY reuse), 7.6-E2E-002, 7.6-BLIND-ERROR-006.

- **DATA-003 (8):** `trade_state=NOTPAY/USERPAYING` must map to `EventUnhandled` (200 ACK, no credit) — crediting an in-progress result risks a never-landing payment. Tests: 7.6-UNIT-010/011, 7.6-INT-015.

## Risk Mitigation Roadmap

### Immediate Actions (Before / Early Development)
1. **SEC-001:** Implement the AES-256-GCM decrypt with stdlib `gcm.Open` (no manual tag split) + the fail-closed adversarial lane (tampered ciphertext / nonce-AAD / wrong-key) FIRST (TDD), before any happy-path parse.
2. **SEC-002:** Verify-before-decrypt asymmetric `VerifyWebhook` with the adversarial unit lane (forged/absent/wrong-key/**unknown-serial**/tamper) before any decrypt or mapping; an unknown `Wechatpay-Serial` MUST reject.
3. **DATA-001:** EXTEND the 7.5 per-currency minor-unit table with HKD + fail-closed parse + string-decimal end-to-end, with round-trip + fail-closed tests.
4. **SEC-004:** Assert the call order — a forged signature is rejected BEFORE `gcm.Open` is reached (no decrypt oracle).

### During Development
1. **SEC-003:** `Wechatpay-Timestamp` freshness (inject `WithClock`) — both replay layers.
2. **DATA-002:** Drive the inherited `toUSD()` for HKD (Q-FX-INTENT paid-currency comparison) with **ZERO `credit.go` change**; seed an HKD `fx_rate` row + missing-rate fail-closed park.
3. **TECH-002 / TECH-003:** Assert gateway header/body pass-through (4 Wechatpay-* headers) + seam parity (no downstream `"wechat"` branch; credit.go untouched).
4. **SEC-005 / SEC-006:** Secret-discipline assertion (neither the merchant private key NOR the `APIV3Key` in logs/errors).

### Post-Development
1. **OPS:** Wire `he_payment_recharge_total{provider="wechat"}`, `he_payment_credit_amount_usd{provider="wechat"}`, and `payment_webhook_rejected{provider="wechat"}` to dashboards/alerts.
2. **Docs:** security.md §8.4 (APIv3 asymmetric-verify + **AES-GCM resource-decrypt** + wallet no-card), tech-stack §2.1, rest-api-spec §5.1.3, service-topology §3.2 (5th/FINAL channel).

## Compliance and Regulatory Considerations

- **PCI-DSS (SAQ-A):** WeChat Pay is a wallet/account channel; the user authenticates in their WeChat app via the Native QR (`code_url`). NO card PAN transits He-API (we persist only the WeChat `transaction_id` + our `out_trade_no`). The SAQ-A boundary established in 7.3 holds (BUS-001).
- **Financial integrity:** Exactly-once credit + amount-integrity + fail-closed scaling/fx/decrypt are the load-bearing controls; all map to P0 tests. The AES-GCM auth tag is a second integrity layer under the asymmetric signature.
- **Secret handling:** TWO high-value secrets — the merchant private key (broad blast radius) AND the symmetric `APIV3Key` (decrypts every callback) — are env-injected, never logged; the platform cert rotates via `Wechatpay-Serial` (config-managed for MVP).
