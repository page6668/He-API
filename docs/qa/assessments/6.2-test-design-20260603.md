The framework's "E2E" tier (user journeys, visual regression) does not apply. The end-to-end *proof* is delivered at the **integration** level via testcontainers (gateway ↔ routing-svc ↔ seeded PostgreSQL) — scenarios `6.2-INT-009…014`. Decision 8B's `test_levels: [unit, integration, contract]` is honoured; the contract tier is the `buf breaking` additive-field gate (`6.2-INT-015`).

**Project type**: backend Go monolith (two services: `api-gateway`, `routing-svc`). Blind-spot high-priority categories (backend_api): BOUNDARY, ERROR, CONCURRENCY, DATA, RESOURCE; medium: FLOW.
