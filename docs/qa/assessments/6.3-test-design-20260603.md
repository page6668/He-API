**Project type**: backend Go monolith (two services: `api-gateway`, `routing-svc`). Blind-spot high-priority categories (backend_api): BOUNDARY, ERROR, CONCURRENCY, DATA, RESOURCE; medium: FLOW.

**Architect Round 1 (Wright, 9.5/10) LOCKED all Q-A…Q-L** — the scope below is fully ratified, no open forks. Two low items carried into design: **m-1** (failover loop wraps at the `ServeHTTP`/`serveStream` Resolve→dispatch boundary, not strictly inside `serveAdapter*`) → scenarios `6.3-UNIT-027/034`; **m-2** (integer-aligned `[1,2,3]` histogram buckets for attempts-count) → scenario `6.3-UNIT-044`.
