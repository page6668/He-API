### Why "Comprehensive" (not Simple or Standard)

Decision 8B locks **Comprehensive** because:
- Rule 1 (Simple) fails: `complexity_indicators = 3 > 0`.
- Rule 2 (Standard) fails: `complexity_indicators = 3 > 2`.
- Rule 3 (Comprehensive) matches: `complexity_indicators ≥ 3` → unit + integration + e2e mandated.
- Rule 4 (Comprehensive via `security_sensitive`) NOT triggered: Story 3.6's security surface is wholly inherited; `security_sensitive = false` per metadata.

Comprehensive level routes ~38 canonical codes through the unified writer, refactors 7 divergent error sites, and lands a new cross-cutting middleware on every /v1/* surface — the test budget MUST cover (a) byte-exact envelope shape on every refactored call-site, (b) the OTel-derived derivation rule + crypto/rand fallback, (c) the matrix-smoke regression across every endpoint the gateway exposes, and (d) the SDK-tier contract. No separate risk-profile document is required (security_focus=false), but the implicit risk surface R1–R12 is enumerated below.
