### Architect-issue assertions folded in

- **M-1 (Decimal-not-float trap):** `7.1-UNIT-014` is the dedicated float-slip golden vector — a vector that `float64` would mis-round but `shopspring/decimal` HALF-UP gets byte-exact. The whole UNIT-004…013 golden suite asserts byte-exact `NUMERIC(12,4)`.
- **M-2 (cap-counter precision mismatch):** `7.1-INT-012` asserts the coarse `api_keys.current_month_cost_usd NUMERIC(10,2)` increment is **not** the reconciliation target; `7.1-E2E-001` reconciles `SUM(usage_ledger.cost_usd)` against `balances.current_usd` (both `NUMERIC(12,4)`).
- **Low-issue (round-to-zero):** `7.1-UNIT-011` is the explicit sub-0.5-mill → `0.0000` vector.
