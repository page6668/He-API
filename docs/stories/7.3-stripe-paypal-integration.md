## Architect Open Questions (gathered for Architect Round 1)

```yaml
Q-SVC:          # MOST CONSEQUENTIAL — service decomposition
  question: "Does the Stripe/PayPal integration land in a NEW `payment-svc` (per service-topology §3.2 + source-tree §6) or fold into the existing `billing-svc`?"
  sm_default: "NEW `payment-svc` (FIRST realisation — the topology + source-tree already name it but it was never built, mirroring 7.1 first-realising billing-svc / 6.1 routing-svc). topology §3.2 explicitly splits payment-svc (`5 通道支付集成、Webhook 处理、对账`) from billing-svc (`计费引擎、订单、订阅、退款、余额扣减`). payment-svc owns the provider SDKs + webhook signature verification + the provider secrets (blast-radius isolation); billing-svc stays the money-ledger authority. They communicate via the `payment.completed` Kafka topic (data-models §4.4 — already lists billing-svc + notification-svc as consumers)."
  why: "Isolating the provider SDKs + secrets + the inbound webhook surface in a dedicated service keeps billing-svc's money-correctness core free of third-party-API blast radius and matches the documented topology verbatim. The alternative (fold into billing-svc) is fewer moving parts but couples payment-provider churn to the ledger service and contradicts the topology."
Q-SUBSCOPE:     # subscription-tier boundary with Story 7.8
  question: "Does 7.3 ship subscription TIERS (Free/Pro/Team/Enterprise catalog + entitlements + Beta gate) or only the recurring-charge RAIL that 7.8 builds on?"
  sm_default: "RAIL ONLY. 7.3 delivers: create a provider subscription (Stripe Subscription / PayPal subscription), persist a `subscriptions` row, and handle the recurring `invoice.paid` / subscription-active webhook → mark subscriptions.status=active/renewed. The TIER CATALOG (plan definitions, entitlement mapping, upgrade/downgrade, Beta sandbox) is Story 7.8 (epic-7 YAML: 7.8 = `订阅档（Free/Pro/Team/Enterprise）+ Beta 模式开关`). The epic AC clause `订阅扣款` = the payment rail that charges a subscription; the tier semantics on top are 7.8."
  why: "epic-7 explicitly scopes tiers to 7.8. Building the catalog/entitlement logic here would pre-empt 7.8 and double the surface. RAIL-only keeps 7.3 a payment-integration story. NEEDS RULING on what a paid subscription GRANTS in 7.3 before 7.8 exists — SM default: it credits/marks active only; entitlement is 7.8."
Q-WEBHOOK-INGRESS:
  question: "Do inbound webhooks hit the api-gateway (proxied to payment-svc) or payment-svc directly via its own ingress?"
  sm_default: "api-gateway hosts the public webhook routes (single public ingress, mounted OUTSIDE bearer auth — 4.7 /public/* precedent) and forwards the RAW request body + signature header to payment-svc for verification. CRITICAL: signature verification needs the EXACT raw bytes — the gateway MUST NOT re-serialize/normalize the body before forwarding (raw-body-integrity)."
  why: "One public ingress surface is simpler to secure + observe. But raw-body integrity is non-negotiable for HMAC verification — if the gateway can't pass bytes through verbatim, payment-svc needs its own ingress. Architect rules on the ingress topology + the raw-body pass-through mechanism."
Q-ORDEROWNER:
  question: "Who WRITES recharge_orders rows — billing-svc (sole writer) or payment-svc?"
  sm_default: "billing-svc is the SOLE writer (topology §3.2: billing-svc owns `订单`), realising the §5.2-sketched-but-deferred `BillingService.CreateRechargeOrder` gRPC. payment-svc calls it to create the PENDING order (gets back our internal order id → passes it as provider metadata); on a verified webhook, payment-svc emits payment.completed{order_id, external_order_id, settled_amount, status}; billing-svc consumes → flips pending→paid + credits. Single-writer mirrors the auth-svc-sole-writer-of-api_keys pattern."
  why: "Single-writer keeps the recharge_orders state-machine + credit atomicity in ONE service (billing-svc) where the balance also lives — the credit + status-flip can be ONE PG tx. Alternative: payment-svc owns recharge_orders directly (fewer hops, but splits the order state from the balance across two services → distributed-tx risk on credit)."
Q-CREDIT-IDEMPOTENCY:
  question: "Exactly-once credit fence shape?"
  sm_default: "recharge_orders.status pending→paid state-machine: `UPDATE ... SET status='paid', paid_at=NOW() WHERE id=$1 AND status='pending'` — zero rows updated ⇒ already-credited ⇒ skip (idempotent). PLUS a NEW UNIQUE(payment_provider, external_order_id) constraint (beyond the §4.1 plain index) so two orders can't bind one provider charge. status-flip + balance-credit in ONE PG tx (7.1 ledger+deduct atomicity cascade)."
  why: "Mirrors the 7.1 exactly-once debit (usage_ledger ON CONFLICT) on the CREDIT side. The state-machine guard is the load-bearing idempotency fence for webhook redelivery."
Q-AMOUNT:
  question: "Which amount is credited — the client-supplied create-order amount or the provider-confirmed settled amount?"
  sm_default: "the PROVIDER-CONFIRMED settled amount from the verified webhook (the money the provider actually captured), NEVER the client create-order intent. recharge_orders.amount is the INTENT (display/UX); the credited value is the provider TRUTH. A settled≠intent mismatch → park + alert, do NOT credit (anti-tampering)."
  why: "Crediting the client-asserted amount is an amount-tampering fraud vector. The provider's signed webhook is the only trustworthy amount."
Q-CURRENCY:
  question: "How does a non-USD (CNY) recharge credit the USD single-SoT balance (7.2 Q-SOT)?"
  sm_default: "the credit ALWAYS lands in balances.current_usd. **Business Rules**
```
| ID | Rule |
|----|------|
| BR-R-1 | The CREDIT amount is the **provider-confirmed settled amount** from the verified webhook, NEVER the client-supplied create-order amount (Q-AMOUNT). recharge_orders.amount is the INTENT; the credited value is the provider TRUTH. A settled≠intent mismatch → park + alert, NO credit. |
| BR-R-2 | **USD is the single accounting SoT** (7.1/7.2 Q-SOT). The credit ALWAYS lands in balances.current_usd; a non-USD payment is converted to USD-equivalent at the 7.2 fx_rate at settlement (Q-CURRENCY). current_rmb stays reserved/0. The 7.1 reconciliation invariant is EXTENDED (debits + credits) not fractured. |
| BR-R-3 | **No card data ever touches He-API** (PCI §8.4, SAQ-A). Payment is provider-hosted (Stripe Checkout redirect / PayPal) + client-side tokenization; we store only the provider token + recharge_orders id. No endpoint accepts a PAN/CVV. |
| BR-R-4 | The recharge_orders row binds order→user SERVER-SIDE at create time (user from the authenticated session, NOT client-supplied); the webhook resolves the user via OUR order id (carried as provider metadata), never a client-asserted user_id (anti cross-user binding). |
| BR-R-5 | All money fields on every billing/payment REST response are **string-decimals** (`"50.00"`) — Q-Spec-4 / 7.1 BR-A-3 cascade; a JSON-number money field is a contract violation. |
| BR-R-6 | The create-order call uses a provider **Idempotency-Key** (Q-SDK) so a client double-submit does not create two provider charges. |
| BR-R-7 | Stripe and PayPal satisfy the SAME `PaymentProvider` seam (Q-PROVIDER-SEAM); the recharge handler is provider-agnostic. 7.4-7.6 add impls without changing the handler. |

**Data Validation**
| Field | Type | Required | Rules | Error Message |
|-------|------|----------|-------|---------------|
| amount | string-decimal | yes | parses to NUMERIC(12,4); `> 0`; ≤ a sane ceiling (Q — anti-abuse) | `400_invalid_payment_request` |
| currency | string | yes | ∈ enabled set (Q-CURRENCY; MVP {USD} +/- {CNY}) | `400_unsupported_currency` (reuse 7.2 code) |
| provider | string | yes | ∈ {stripe, paypal} for 7.3 | `400_unsupported_payment_provider` |

**Error Handling**
| Scenario | Code/Action |
|----------|-------------|
| amount ≤ 0 / unparseable | `400_invalid_payment_request` — reject before provider call |
| provider ∉ {stripe,paypal} | `400_unsupported_payment_provider` (USDC/Alipay/WeChat are 7.4-7.6) |

### AC3: webhook 安全 + 精确一次入账 — signature verification + exactly-once credit backbone (安全 + 准确)

**Scenario**
```gherkin
GIVEN
  - POST /v1/billing/webhooks/stripe + POST /v1/billing/webhooks/paypal are mounted OUTSIDE the bearer/JWT chain (4.7 /public/* precedent) — the provider SIGNATURE is the sole credential
  - the Stripe webhook signing secret + PayPal webhook id are configured (Q-SECRETS); raw-body integrity is preserved end-to-end (Q-WEBHOOK-INGRESS)
  - recharge_orders + subscriptions carry the idempotency fences (Q-CREDIT-IDEMPOTENCY)
WHEN
  - a webhook POST arrives (genuine, forged, replayed, or amount-tampered)
THEN
  - **genuine** (valid signature, fresh timestamp): Stripe → verify HMAC-SHA256 over `timestamp.payload` with the signing secret (constant-time compare; reject outside the timestamp tolerance); PayPal → verify transmission signature (cert-chain / verify-webhook-signature). On success → process the event (credit / renew); return 2xx promptly
  - **forged** (invalid/absent/tampered signature): reject with `400_webhook_signature_invalid` BEFORE parsing the body into any money action; NO credit; slog payment_webhook_rejected{reason:'signature'} + metric; the body is NEVER trusted
  - **replayed** (valid-but-stale or duplicate): the Stripe timestamp-tolerance rejects a stale signature; a duplicate of a still-fresh event is made harmless by the recharge_orders pending→paid state-machine (zero-row UPDATE → no second credit) — defence in depth (freshness + idempotent apply)
  - **exactly-once credit**: the credit applies ONLY on a pending→paid transition (UPDATE ... WHERE status='pending'); already-paid ⇒ zero rows ⇒ ACK + skip; the status-flip + balance-credit are ONE PG tx (atomic); UNIQUE(payment_provider, external_order_id) prevents two orders binding one charge
  - **amount integrity** (Q-AMOUNT): the credited amount is the provider-verified settled amount, not the order intent; a settled≠intent → park + alert + NO credit
  - **reconciliation invariant**: SUM(recharge_orders.amount WHERE status='paid' AND user=U, in USD-equiv) + SUM(subscription credits) − SUM(usage_ledger.cost_usd WHERE user=U) reconciles against balances.current_usd within rounding tolerance (extends the 7.1 debit-only invariant to debit+credit)
```

**Business Rules**
| ID | Rule |
|----|------|
| BR-W-1 | **Signature verification is MANDATORY and happens BEFORE any body processing.** An unverified webhook body is NEVER parsed into a credit/renew action. The signature is the credential (the route is unauthenticated otherwise). |
| BR-W-2 | **Raw-body integrity** (Q-WEBHOOK-INGRESS): HMAC verification requires the EXACT raw bytes the provider signed; no middleware (gateway proxy included) may re-serialize/normalize the body before verification. |
| BR-W-3 | **Exactly-once credit** is enforced by the recharge_orders pending→paid state-machine (UPDATE ... WHERE status='pending' → zero rows ⇒ skip) PLUS UNIQUE(payment_provider, external_order_id). The status-flip + balance-credit are ONE PG tx (7.1 atomicity cascade). |
| BR-W-4 | **Replay defence in depth**: provider timestamp-tolerance (signature freshness) + idempotent apply (state-machine). Neither alone is relied upon. |
| BR-W-5 | **Provider 2xx discipline**: a verified event that is already-processed still returns 2xx (so the provider stops retrying); a verification FAILURE returns 400; a transient processing error (e.g. PG down) returns 5xx so the provider redelivers (at-least-once + idempotent apply). |
| BR-W-6 | **Secrets** (Q-SECRETS): the webhook signing secret is DISTINCT from the API secret key; env-injected; NEVER logged (slog redaction, 7.2 cascade). |
| BR-W-7 | **PCI boundary** (§8.4): the webhook payload carries provider tokens + amounts + ids — NEVER a card PAN; we persist provider token + order id only. |

**Error Handling**
| Scenario | Code | Message (en) | Action |
|----------|------|--------------|--------|
| Invalid / absent / tampered signature | 400 | `400_webhook_signature_invalid` — "Webhook signature verification failed." | reject before body processing (BR-W-1); slog + metric |
| Stale signature (outside timestamp tolerance) | 400 | `400_webhook_signature_invalid` | reject (replay defence, BR-W-4) |
| Verified but already-processed (redelivery) | 200 | — | ACK + skip; no second credit (BR-W-3/W-5) |
| Verified but PG down on apply | 5xx | — | do NOT ACK; provider redelivers (BR-W-5) |
| settled_amount ≠ order intent | 200 | — | ACK (don't make provider retry a verified event) but park + alert + NO credit (BR-W-3 / Q-AMOUNT) |

**Examples**
- **Input**: forged POST with a bogus Stripe-Signature → `400_webhook_signature_invalid`; no recharge_orders touched; no credit. ✅ forgery blocked.
- **Input**: genuine `payment_intent.succeeded` for a pending order → verified → pending→paid + balance +amount (one tx). Redelivered → already-paid → 200 ACK, no second credit. ✅ exactly-once.
- **Input**: replayed stale webhook (timestamp 1h old) → outside tolerance → `400_webhook_signature_invalid`. ✅ replay blocked.

### Database Design (WHY, not full SQL)
- **recharge_orders is the order state-machine + credit idempotency fence**: status pending→paid (or failed/refunded). The credit applies ONLY on the pending→paid transition; a redelivered webhook finds paid → no second credit (Q-CREDIT-IDEMPOTENCY). The §4.1 plain idx(provider, external_order_id) is UPGRADED to a UNIQUE constraint so two orders cannot bind one provider charge. [→ data-models §4.1 lines 107-119]
- **subscriptions is the subscription lifecycle SoT** (active/past_due/cancelled per §4.1); 7.3 writes it (RAIL), 7.8 reads it (TIERS). idx(provider, external_subscription_id) serves webhook lookup. [→ data-models §4.1 lines 61-71]
- **Both tables were pre-defined in §4.1 and explicitly NOT created by 7.1** ("recharge_orders / subscriptions tables are NOT created here (Stories 7.8 / 7.3+)"). 0010 is the correct next migration (0009 is HEAD).
- **No card columns** — PCI §8.4; only provider token + ids. `balances.auto_recharge_*` columns (§4.1) exist but are UNTOUCHED (Story 7.7).
